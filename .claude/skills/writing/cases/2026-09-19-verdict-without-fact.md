# A sentence that only gives a verdict, then a rule in place of the problem

2026-09-19. Trial of the first version of the skill, the same chat reply as
`2026-09-19-cost-with-two-meanings.md`.

## The text that was sent

> The faer 6 thread column is not free either. The design rule is rayon
> across records and BLAS inside the products, never nested, and multi
> threaded faer puts faer's own rayon pool inside ours.

## The owner's words

> It would be clearer as: "Having multi threaded faer could bring problems
> because both and BLAS have their own thread pools and their interaction
> has been never tested."
> This sentence "The faer 6 thread column is not free either." conveys no
> meaning, it reads like advertisment speak, but has no meaning beyond the
> valorative.

## What the skill lacked

The list under "Only what the reader can use" had sentences about the text
and about the reader's reaction, not the sentence that only gives a
verdict. And nothing said that a problem is written as what can go wrong
and not as the rule it breaks. Both added, the second under "Write the
contents, not the name of the category".

## To test a change

In the text produced for the same task, the paragraph about the threads
says what could go wrong and that it has not been tested, and no sentence
can be deleted without losing a fact.
